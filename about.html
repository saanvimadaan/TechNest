THE SHORT VERSION

Built by three people annoyed with return policies

TechNest began in a two-bedroom flat with a spreadsheet, a stack of returned earbuds, and a shared frustration: every electronics store made warranty claims feel like a punishment for buying something that broke.

We started small — curating a tight list of products we'd actually vouch for, and answering support emails ourselves. That habit stuck. Every device on TechNest today still goes through the same filter: would we tell a friend to buy this?

We're still not the biggest catalog in electronics, and we're fine with that. We'd rather be the one you trust.
