Frequently asked

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship within 24 hours and arrive in 2–4 business days. Same-day delivery is available in 18 cities at checkout.

What does the 2-year warranty actually cover?

Manufacturing defects and hardware failure under normal use, full stop. No usage logs to submit, no repair-shop runaround — contact us and we'll sort a replacement or repair.

Can I return something after opening it?

Yes — you have 30 days from delivery, opened or not. We just ask that it's in resellable condition with the original accessories.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet — we currently ship within India only, but international shipping is on our roadmap for next year.

How do I track an existing order?

Use the order number from your confirmation email in the contact form above, and we'll pull up the latest status for you.
